=== PAGE 7 ===
Introduction

You are given a music catalog containing 114,000 track records. Suppose a team ultimately
wants to use this dataset to build machine-learning systems for tasks such as predicting track
popularity. Before deciding what models to build, the team first needs to understand the
dataset itself:

 ●   Is the dataset reliable enough to use as training data?
 ●  What structure is present in the audio-feature space?
 ●  Which patterns appear to reflect meaningful properties of the tracks, and which may
     instead result from the way the dataset was collected or represented?
 ●  Which target variables can be predicted meaningfully from the available audio features,
    and which cannot?

Your goal in this homework is to answer these questions with evidence.
